Bryce J. Carpenter and Pieterjan Dierickx discuss how cytosolic NADPH links exercise to physiological cardiac hypertrophy in their new News & Views article, [Increasing cytosolic NADPH to mimic exercise](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01580-2), published in *Nature Metabolism* on 8 September 2026.

**Keywords:** Cardiac metabolism, Exercise, NADPH, Hypertrophy.
